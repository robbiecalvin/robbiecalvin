Questions about site care

Can I ask for a one-off update or content fix?

Yes. Focused support is for a defined WordPress task, with the scope and estimate agreed before work starts.

Does the monthly plan include hosting or emergency repairs?

Hosting, premium licenses, emergency response, malware removal, and major repairs are quoted separately.

What should I include in an enquiry?

Include the site URL, the changes or maintenance you need, and any deadline. Mention whether you already have backups and a staging copy. Send access details only after we agree on the work and how to share credentials.
