Privacy Policy

How Robbie Calvin handles account, service request, app, technical, and payment information, and how to make a privacy request.

Effective and last updated: November 3, 2026

Who is responsible

Robbie Calvin is responsible for personal information handled through robbiecalvin.com and Robbie-controlled services that link to this policy. Send privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or complaints to robbiecalvin8@gmail.com. Use “Privacy request” in the subject so it can be routed clearly. You can also use the contact details on the contact page.

This policy distinguishes the public portfolio, account and order service, client work, and individual app features. A linked third-party site is responsible for its own processing. A specific notice shown inside a feature adds detail about that feature; it does not authorize unrelated uses of your information.

App-specific information

QuickWoo and WordPress plugins: the websites, store records, credentials, configuration, and files you connect can contain customer or business information. Connect only systems you are authorized to manage, grant the permissions needed for the chosen feature, and review that package’s setup and data-handling documentation. Installing a plugin on your own website does not make Robbie the operator of that website or replace your own duties to its visitors and customers.

Typing School: the documented browser version saves practice progress locally and offers exports. Clearing browser storage can remove local progress. KIMIX works with the audio files and recording features you choose; SpeechFlow works with voice capture and saved idea maps. Local files, browser microphone permission, and any service a particular build connects to are separate choices. A portfolio description is not consent to upload a file or activate a microphone.

Red Door: the app’s social features can involve profiles, postings, messages, and location-related choices. Information you publish or send is visible to the audience you choose in that feature. Do not assume a public posting or message recipient will keep it private. Myra uses the preferences or requests you provide for recommendations; following a retailer link takes you to that retailer’s service. Codex Master workflows run in the development environment you choose; protect credentials and confidential code in that environment.

These descriptions identify the feature-related information you choose to use; they do not claim that every version has the same storage, providers, or telemetry. Review the running app’s notices and permissions before using a connected feature. Ask Robbie about a specific build if its data path is unclear. Personal information is not licensed for unrelated marketing or public reuse by submitting it to an app.

Cookies, local storage, and third-party requests

The account service uses secure, HTTP-only session cookies to keep you signed in and refresh an authorized session. Blocking essential cookies prevents account features from working. Sign out and use your browser’s site-data controls to remove stored cookies. Local application data and exports remain on your device unless you choose a feature that sends them elsewhere.

The current public-site build does not include advertising pixels or a marketing analytics script. This is not a claim that hosting providers keep no technical logs. Some page fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the network request needed to deliver them. Public source and assets are hosted using GitHub infrastructure, and Cloudflare delivers and protects the site. External links do not send your local project brief merely because they appear on a page.

Payments and service providers

The payment integration is currently in Stripe sandbox mode. Do not enter real payment credentials into a test checkout. When a real Stripe-hosted checkout is offered, payment information goes directly to Stripe. This website’s order database stores payment references and status, not your full card number or card security code. Stripe may also process information for its own fraud-prevention and legal purposes.

Providers used to operate the service include Cloudflare for delivery/security and API routing, GitHub for public source and site assets, Supabase for authentication and order storage, and Stripe for hosted checkout and payment events. Email correspondence is handled through the email service used for the published contact address. These providers process information needed for their role; their own policies also describe their independent operations.

The current Supabase account/order project is hosted in the United States. Providers and their support or subprocessors may process information in Canada, the United States, or other countries. Information can therefore be subject to lawful access under the laws of those places. Do not use the service on the assumption that all information remains in Canada.

Provider information: Cloudflare privacy, GitHub privacy, Supabase privacy, and Stripe privacy.

Use, disclosure, and your choices

Information is used for the feature or work you request, account security, support, service administration, and legal obligations. It is not sold or rented, and a service request or account registration is not a marketing subscription. Transactional account and order messages are separate from optional promotional messages. If promotional messages are introduced, the required consent and unsubscribe choices must be provided.

Access is limited to Robbie and people or providers who need it to perform the relevant work. Information may also be disclosed with your permission, as required by law, or where legally permitted to investigate fraud, protect people or systems, or address a legal claim. Private client submissions are not published as portfolio examples without permission.

You may decline optional information, stop using a feature, revoke browser permissions, or request withdrawal of consent where applicable. This can affect whether the requested service can be delivered. Withdrawing consent does not require deletion of records that must lawfully be retained.

Retention and security

Account records are retained to operate the account. Project, order, correspondence, and payment-reference records are retained for the work, support, accounting, dispute handling, and legal obligations that apply. Local drafts and exports stay under your control. There is no promise that all copies disappear instantly after a request: required records and provider backups can remain for their applicable retention period. Ask for deletion or account closure by email; Robbie will explain any record that must be retained and why.

Safeguards include HTTPS, restricted database access, authenticated account requests, secure session cookies, and verification of Stripe payment-event signatures. No system can guarantee perfect security. Protect your device and account credentials and report suspected unauthorized access promptly. Security concerns can be sent privately using the process on the legal page.

Access, correction, questions, and changes

Contact Robbie to request access to your personal information, understand its uses or disclosures, correct an error, request account closure, or raise a concern. Provide enough detail to locate the record, without sending an identity document unless an appropriate verification method has first been agreed. Reasonable identity checks may be needed to avoid releasing information to the wrong person. Applicable legal exceptions can limit access or deletion; a refusal will be explained as required.

If a privacy complaint is not resolved, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia. Other privacy authorities and rights may apply to your circumstances. Policy changes will be dated here, with additional notice or consent where required for a materially different use. A new policy does not retroactively authorize unrelated use of information already supplied.
