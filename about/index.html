01 / A little about me

Practical tools.
Creative expression.

I’m a tech artist, web developer, and AI enthusiast. My work lives where practical tools meet creative expression.

Sometimes that means writing code. Sometimes it means designing an identity, building an AI workflow, or telling a story. I’m interested in what happens when those disciplines start talking to each other.

This is the home for that work: the ideas taking shape, the experiments worth following, and the things I’m still figuring out.

Make it useful.

Good technology should earn its place in someone’s life.

Make it feel.

Function matters. So does the experience of being there.
