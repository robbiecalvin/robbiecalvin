Vancouver / Self-taught / Still becoming

I found my way
by making things.

I’m Robbie Calvin. Programmer, artist, and a believer in what people can build when they get another chance.

This is my story ↓ Say hello ↗
No straight line

I didn’t arrive here with a degree, a plan, or a tidy story.
I arrived with curiosity—and the need to find a way forward.

01

The uncertain part

I didn’t always know
where I was going.

School wasn’t a place where I really applied myself. There was a lot of dysfunction in my life, and for a long time I didn’t know what I wanted to do with it.

College wasn’t something I could afford, and I didn’t have family to lean on. I had to find a direction I could begin with what I had.

I wanted something I could teach myself. Something with enough room to keep changing, where I could learn at my own pace and keep finding new possibilities.

02

The first websites

It started with a couple
of cannabis websites.

Running those sites gave me a reason to figure things out. I needed to understand how websites worked, how to change them, and how to make them do something useful.

That curiosity snowballed. A question led to something to learn. Learning led to another idea. Eventually, I found myself programming—building the tools and experiences I once wouldn’t have known how to begin.

I’m self-taught. That means I’m used to asking questions, trying things, and staying with a problem until I understand it a little better.

There was no perfect starting point.
There was something I could learn next.

03 / The artist never left

Code is another medium.

Drawing Painting Pastels Programming

I draw, paint, and work with pastels. Making art has its own way of helping me explore an idea, follow a feeling, or notice something I might otherwise pass by.

Programming gives me another way to make things. I can work with structure and interaction as well as colour and form. That overlap is where a lot of my curiosity lives.

04 / What I keep coming back to

Build something
that helps someone.

01 / Possibility

AI used for good.

I believe AI is part of the future. I’m interested in what people can do with it: learn, create, solve problems, and build things that make life better.

02 / Another chance

Recovery matters.

I believe in recovery, in people’s ability to change, and in the value of having a way forward. A difficult chapter shouldn’t get to define the whole story.

03 / A voice

Help and advocacy.

Helping people and standing up for them matter to me. I want that care to show up in the work I choose and in how I work with others.

04 / Expression

Keep making.

Creativity is a thread through all of it. A drawing, a useful website, a new tool—different ways to turn an idea into something someone else can experience.

Vancouver is home. Curiosity is the direction.

I’m still learning.
I’m still making things.

If something here speaks to you, I’d like to hear about it.
