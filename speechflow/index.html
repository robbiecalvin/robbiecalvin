01 / The experience

Speak it. Map it. Play it back.

SpeechFlow turns voice capture into a visual thinking workspace. Arrange draggable bubbles, connect ideas with directed links, and use playback to follow your thinking through the map.

01 /

Capture a thought

Use voice capture as a starting point for a visual map of the ideas you want to explore.

02 /

Make the connections

Move bubbles around the workspace and connect them with directed links to give your thinking a visible structure.

03 /

Follow the flow

Use playback to revisit the map. Legacy snapshot migration helps keep older saved maps loadable as the workspace evolves.

02 / How it fits together

Room to move your ideas around.

Drag-end collision resolution helps separate nodes, while cluster-aware zoom-to-fit helps bring a group of ideas back into view.

03 / Explore the project

Start with the browser workspace

The README describes running SpeechFlow in a modern Chromium-based browser, either by opening the entry page or serving the project locally. See the repository for setup and version notes.
