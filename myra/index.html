01 / The experience

Find your next wine or beer.

Myra is a virtual sommelier for wine and beer. Use a guided flow or request mode to explore recommendations, compare the top three results, and move toward a retailer when you find something that interests you.

01 /

Choose your own starting point

Follow the guided experience or use request mode to explore wine and beer recommendations.

02 /

Compare the top three

Wine and beer results share a consistent top-three card layout, making recommendations easier to browse and compare.

03 /

Take the next step

A retailer handoff section is integrated into the results flow, connecting discovery with a next step beyond the recommendation.

02 / How it fits together

A sommelier in your browser.

The web experience adapts across desktop, tablet, and mobile. The repository also includes an Expo mobile app targeting Android and iOS.
