Before choosing a plan

A few useful distinctions.

What does Lite include?

Each individual module is listed with a free Lite edition. The matrix does not specify its exact feature limits or release downloads. Feature descriptions on these pages refer to the documented full builds; they are not a promise that every feature is included in Lite.

How are annual prices shown?

Annual plans show the full prepaid USD total for a year, using the workbook’s stated 20% discount. They are not monthly charges. Agency covers up to 25 sites, not unlimited sites. Taxes are excluded.

What is included with Desktop?

The Full Suite + QuickWoo Desktop plan includes all 18 Pro modules and the desktop app. It requires licensed connector/server functionality. The plugin-only Full Suite does not include Desktop in the matrix.

Can I buy or download a license now?

These are proposed launch prices. Live subscription checkout, license delivery, and the final release downloads have not been connected yet. You can enquire about a plan using the links on its page.

Why is Education the same price as Course Builder?

The Education Bundle contains Quick Course Builder alone in this matrix. Its prices are identical to that plugin’s individual prices; no additional bundle discount is claimed.

What about lifetime licenses?

Lifetime licenses are not offered at launch. The available paid billing options are monthly and prepaid annual.
