01 / The experience

A map. A conversation. A connection.

Red Door is an adults-only, cruise-first social platform built around map discovery, profiles, and conversation. Explore cruising spots, public postings, and groups, with messaging and moderation tools woven into the experience.

01 /

Discover around you

Map and chat discovery views bring profiles, cruising spots, and check-ins into the same social space. Travel mode supports exploring another location.

02 /

Keep the conversation going

Direct messages, group invitations, read receipts, and inbox views help carry a connection beyond discovery.

03 /

Control your experience

Blocking, reporting, moderation, and age-gate flows are documented parts of the platform. Profiles and media have their own ownership controls.

02 / How it fits together

Built around Cruise mode.

The active frontend focuses on Cruise mode. Date and Hybrid logic exists in the backend, but those modes should not be read as fully available frontend experiences.

Explore the project
