01 / The experience

Build speed. Understand your rhythm.

Typing School is a browser-based typing training platform with lessons, practice, speed tests, and a progress dashboard. Version 3 adds targeted lesson generation, finger drills, rhythm analysis, and more ways to understand how you type.

01 /

Practice the weak spots

Targeted paragraphs use your mistake profile, weak keys, and speed. Finger training adds left/right drills and shift-focused exercises.

02 /

See beyond words per minute

Rhythm analysis and session heatmaps examine hesitation, bursts, punctuation slowdowns, per-key delay, and mistake rates.

03 /

Find your way to practice

Try survival, elimination, burst, and marathon modes, developer challenges, and QWERTY, DVORAK, or COLEMAK layouts.

02 / How it fits together

Your progress, on your device.

Progress is stored locally in your browser. Replay controls let you revisit a session, while JSON and CSV exports help you take your stats with you. Offline support is available after the first HTTP load.

03 / Explore the project

Start with the training platform

The project is fully static and runs in the browser without a backend. Open the repository for setup, lessons, and the complete Version 3 feature list. Local progress belongs to that browser rather than a synced account.
